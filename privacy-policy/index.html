Privacy Policy

Last update: 22nd November 2026

Applies to oursuccesscoach.com and everything published under it, including Expression (expression.oursuccesscoach.com) and the client agreement service (agree.oursuccesscoach.com). Current version: oursuccesscoach.com/privacy-policy/

Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to oursuccesscoach.com and to everything published under it, including tools such as Expression, which runs at expression.oursuccesscoach.com, and the client agreement service, which runs at agree.oursuccesscoach.com (together, the "Website"). The Website is run by OUR SUCCESS COACH, UNIPESSOAL LDA ("Our Success Coach", "we", "us"), NIPC 519643143, with its registered office at Rua Principal do Casal Viegas, n.º 228, 3240-214 Ansião, Portugal. We are the controller of personal data processed through the Website. You can reach us at tim@oursuccesscoach.com.

We handle personal data in line with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation) and Portuguese data protection law.

What we collect and why

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to reply and to keep a record of the correspondence. Our mail is hosted by Apple (iCloud).

Technical data. The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Expression and the client agreement service are served from a server operated by us in Portugal, reached through a relay server that we rent from Hetzner in Finland. The relay passes your connection to our server; it is set up not to record IP addresses or the pages requested. When you visit the Website itself, GitHub receives your IP address and the details of your request (such as the page, browser and time) and may keep server logs. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking tools on the Website.

Expression. Expression is a tool from Our Success Coach used to collaborate with teams interested in continuous improvement. When you join a session through a link, we store on our own server the display name you choose, the cards you write, your votes, and when you joined and last took part, so that everyone with the link sees the same session. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your email address. Your browser also keeps, in its local storage, a random identifier and secret that let you return to your own cards, along with your name and display preferences. The host who creates sessions signs in with a passkey; the passkey's public key and a record of the host's sign-in sessions are stored on the same server. Expression does not use advertising or analytics.

To protect the service from abuse, the server briefly holds your IP address in memory to limit repeated requests. It is not written to the Expression data and is discarded within minutes.

Client agreements. If we invite you or your organisation to accept an agreement, such as a Master Services Agreement, Statement of Work or Change Order, we send you a private link to agree.oursuccesscoach.com. The agreement service is used only by people we have invited. When you accept an agreement through it, we collect and store on our own server your name, job title, company name and number, and email address, the date and time of each step, your IP address and browser details, and the exact text you accepted together with a cryptographic hash of it. We send you a link to confirm your acceptance by email and then email a copy of the agreement and a certificate of completion, as a PDF, to you and to ourselves. We use this data to make and perform the agreement and to keep evidence that it was accepted. Our server also keeps ordinary request logs, which include IP addresses, to run and protect the service. The service does not use advertising or analytics.

Clients and business contacts. If you are, or may become, a client of Our Success Coach, or you work for one, we also hold your name, job title, organisation, contact details, contracts and statements of work, invoices and correspondence. We use them to make and perform our agreements, to invoice and to meet accounting and tax duties, and for our legitimate interest in managing the relationship. We share them with our accountant and legal advisers, our email provider and our other service providers as needed, and with authorities where the law requires. We keep them for as long as the agreement lasts and for as long as the law requires, for example for accounting and tax purposes.

Cookies

The Website does not set cookies for visitors, for people who join an Expression session through a link, or for clients who accept an agreement. Third parties that serve content to your browser, such as GitHub, or sites you choose to visit from our links, may set their own.

Who receives your data

We use GitHub (website hosting), Apple (email, including the confirmation and copies sent by the client agreement service), Hetzner (the relay server that carries Expression and client agreement traffic) and Tailscale (the private encrypted link between that relay and our server) as service providers. We have a data processing agreement with Hetzner. Content that passes between them is encrypted in transit. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We may disclose data to authorities or advisers where the law requires it or where needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area

The Expression and client agreement server is in Portugal and the relay is in Finland, both within the European Economic Area. GitHub and Apple are based in the United States, and Tailscale in Canada and the United States. Where they process personal data outside the European Economic Area, they rely on safeguards recognised by EU law: the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for details of the safeguards for a particular provider.

Security

Connections to the Website, Expression and the client agreement service are encrypted in transit. We run the Expression and client agreement servers ourselves. The host of Expression signs in with a passkey, and administration of the client agreement service is possible only over a private connection from our own devices. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights and freedoms at risk, we will notify the CNPD within 72 hours where the law requires, and tell the people affected where the law requires.

Automated decisions and AI

We do not make decisions about you by automated means, and we do not profile you. Expression and the client agreement service do not use artificial intelligence, and we do not use the personal data they hold to train AI models.

How long we keep data

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for any period required by law, for example for accounting and tax purposes. Provider logs are kept according to the provider's own policy.

Accepted agreements and their acceptance records, including the details listed above, are kept for ten years from acceptance, so that we can prove what was agreed and meet our legal obligations, and are then deleted. Invitations that are never accepted are kept only for as long as we need them for that purpose. Backups of this data are kept on our own systems and are replaced on a rolling basis.

Expression sessions, with the names, cards and votes in them, are kept until the host deletes them. On request we will delete a session or the entries you made in it; email us with the session link.

Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email us. For accepted agreements, these rights apply within the limits of the retention period above and of any legal obligation to keep the records. You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), at www.cnpd.pt.
