Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to oursuccesscoach.com and to everything published under it, including tools such as Expression (together, the "Website"). The Website is run by OUR SUCCESS COACH, UNIPESSOAL LDA ("Our Success Coach", "we", "us"), NIPC 519643143, with its registered office at Rua Principal do Casal Viegas, n.º 228, 3240-214 Ansião, Portugal. We are the controller of personal data processed through the Website. You can reach us at tim@oursuccesscoach.com.

We handle personal data in line with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation) and Portuguese data protection law.

What we collect and why

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to reply and to keep a record of the correspondence. Our mail is hosted by Apple (iCloud).

Technical data. The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Expression is served from a server operated by us in Portugal, reached through a relay server that we rent from Hetzner in Finland. The relay passes your connection to our server; it is set up not to record IP addresses or the pages requested. When you visit the Website itself, GitHub receives your IP address and the details of your request (such as the page, browser and time) and may keep server logs. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking tools on the Website.

Fonts. Some pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore sends your IP address and request details to Google when those pages load.

Expression. Expression is a free tool for running team sessions. When you join a session through a link, we store on our own server the display name you choose, the cards you write, your votes, and when you joined and last took part, so that everyone with the link sees the same session. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your email address. Your browser also keeps, in its local storage, a random identifier and secret that let you return to your own cards, along with your name and display preferences. The host who creates sessions signs in with a passkey; the passkey's public key and a record of the host's sign-in sessions are stored on the same server. Expression does not use advertising or analytics.

To protect the service from abuse, the server briefly holds your IP address in memory to limit repeated requests. It is not written to the Expression data and is discarded within minutes.

Cookies

The Website does not set cookies for visitors. When the host signs in to Expression, one strictly necessary cookie named osc_expression_host is set to keep the host signed in; it is used for nothing else, is not available to scripts, is sent only to Expression and expires on its own. People who join a session through a link do not receive it. Third parties that serve content to your browser, such as Google Fonts or GitHub, or sites you choose to visit from our links, may set their own.

Legal basis

We process the data above on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating the Website and answering enquiries, and, where you ask us about our services, to take steps at your request before entering into an agreement. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Who receives your data

We use GitHub (website hosting), Apple (email), Google (fonts), Hetzner (the relay server that carries Expression traffic) and Tailscale (the private encrypted link between that relay and our server) as service providers. Content that passes between them is encrypted in transit. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We may disclose data to authorities or advisers where the law requires it or where needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area

The Expression server is in Portugal and the relay is in Finland, both within the European Economic Area. Some of our other providers process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for any period required by law, for example for accounting and tax purposes. Provider logs are kept according to the provider's own policy.

Expression sessions, with the names, cards and votes in them, are kept until the host deletes them. On request we will delete a session or the entries you made in it; email us with the session link.

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email us. You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), at www.cnpd.pt.

Children
