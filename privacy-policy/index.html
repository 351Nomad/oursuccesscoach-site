We handle personal data in line with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation) and Portuguese data protection law.

What we collect and why

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to reply and to keep a record of the correspondence. Our mail is hosted by Apple (iCloud).

Technical data. The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub receives your IP address and the details of your request (such as the page, browser and time) and may keep server logs. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking tools on the Website.

Fonts. Some pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore sends your IP address and request details to Google when those pages load.

Tools such as Expression. As currently offered, Expression runs entirely in your browser. What you type, your name and your boards are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device, and are not sent to us. If we change a tool so that data is stored on our servers, we will update this policy first and say so in the tool.

Cookies

We do not set cookies. Third parties that serve content to your browser, such as Google Fonts or GitHub, or sites you choose to visit from our links, may set their own.

We use GitHub (website hosting), Apple (email) and Google (fonts) as service providers. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We may disclose data to authorities or advisers where the law requires it or where needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some of these providers process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for any period required by law, for example for accounting and tax purposes. Provider logs are kept according to the provider's own policy.
